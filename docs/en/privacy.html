Privacy Policy of the “Home Under Control” app

Effective date: 16 October 2026.

Developer: Tagirov Tagir, contact: tagir.tagirov.987@gmail.com

This is a translation. If it differs from the Russian original, the Russian original prevails.

In short

The app doesn’t collect your data. Everything you enter is stored only on your device. The app has no servers, accounts, ads or analytics.

The app goes online for one purpose only - to get exchange rates. That request contains none of your records: only currency codes.

What data you enter

• names and dates of deadlines;
• names, amounts and dates of recurring payments;
• amounts, categories, dates and notes of expenses, and for grocery expenses - the types of items bought and their amounts, if you enter them;
• meter names, readings and tariffs;
• your monthly budget;
• savings goals: what you are saving for, how much you need, deposits and withdrawals;
• app settings, including the chosen language, reminder times and the days you marked “Didn’t spend today”.

This data is written to a database on your device. In store builds the database is encrypted; the encryption key is kept in the system’s secure storage and never leaves the device. The developer has no access to your data.

Permissions

• Camera and photos - to take or choose a photo of a meter. The photo is shown on screen so it’s easy to copy the reading; the app doesn’t save it or send it anywhere.
• Microphone and speech recognition - to add an expense by voice. Speech is turned into text by your phone’s system service. If the Russian language model is installed on the device, audio doesn’t leave the device. If not, audio is processed by Google (on Android) or Apple (on iPhone) speech recognition under their own rules. The app receives only text and doesn’t save the recording. Before first use the app shows this explanation and asks for consent; you can withdraw it in settings.
• Notifications - to remind you of deadlines and payments and, if you turned on the evening reminder, once a day - when no expense was added that day - to suggest adding your spending. Notifications are created on the device, without a server. By default they contain no names or amounts; the evening reminder never contains them.
• Biometrics - to open the app if you turned on the lock. The check is done by the system; the app never receives your fingerprint or face image.

Each permission is requested when you use the corresponding feature. Refusing doesn’t stop you from entering data manually.

Internet access

The app goes online only for exchange rates - to show the combined total of expenses in the currency you chose.

• When. When you open the app, if a week has passed since the last successful check or the saved rate is older than a week, and when you tap “Update rate”. There are no requests in the background while the app is closed. Automatic checks can be turned off in settings.
• Where. The Frankfurter service (api.frankfurter.dev); if it’s unavailable - ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). The app has no access keys or accounts with these services.
• What is sent. Only the app’s currency codes (VND, RUB, USD, EUR) in the request address - the same for all users. Your expenses, amounts, notes, names and any other records are not sent.
• What the service may see. The device’s IP address, the time of the request and technical details of the connection - like any website you open. How the services handle this information is governed by their own rules: frankfurter.dev and exchangerate-api.com. The app developer doesn’t receive this information.
• What stays on the device. The rates received, the date of those rates and the time of the last check. Without internet the app works as usual and uses the last saved rate.

Sharing with third parties

The app doesn’t share your data with third parties and doesn’t sell it. The exchange-rate request contains none of your records.

Backups

You can save a copy of your data to a file. Where to send the file is up to you; from then on its safety depends on the place you chose. A backup can be protected with a password. A forgotten password can’t be recovered. Saved exchange rates, the chosen appearance and language, evening reminder settings and “Didn’t spend today” marks are not included in the backup: they belong to this device.

Android system backup is turned off for the app. On iPhone the database file may end up in your iCloud or computer backup if such backups are on; it stays encrypted, and the key isn’t included in the backup.

Deleting data

App settings have “Delete all data”: records and settings are erased from the device permanently. Uninstalling the app also deletes all its data. The app can’t delete backup files you saved earlier - delete them wherever they are.

Children

The app isn’t intended for children under 13 and doesn’t knowingly collect their data - just as it doesn’t collect the data of any other users.

Changes

If the policy changes, the new version will appear at this same link with a new date. The app will tell you about significant changes when it updates.
