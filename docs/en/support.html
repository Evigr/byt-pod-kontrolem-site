Support

Found a bug or have an idea for an improvement? Write to the developer.

tagir.tagirov.987@gmail.com ↗

Developer: Tagirov Tagir

What to include

Include your phone model, Android and app versions, what you were doing and what happened. Do not send passwords or backups containing personal records.

Frequently asked questions

How do I back up my records?

Create a backup in the app’s Settings. Keep the file somewhere safe and protect it with a password if needed.

Can a forgotten backup password be recovered?

No. The developer has no access to your data and cannot recover the password.

Does the app pay bills?

No. It helps you keep records. Marking a bill as paid does not make a bank transfer.

How do I delete my data?

Choose “Delete all data” in the app’s Settings. Delete any separately saved backup files from wherever you stored them.
