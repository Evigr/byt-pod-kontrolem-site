ONE APP. EVERYDAY ESSENTIALS.

Give everyday admin
less of your attention.

01 / DEADLINES

Important dates in sight

Visas, insurance, leases or recurring tasks. Day counters, clear urgency colours and reminders.

02 / EXPENSES

Know where it went

Expenses by day and category, search and a combined total in your main currency. Each purchase keeps its original amount.

03 / BILLS

Keep payments clear

Recurring payments, payment history and meter readings. Fixed charges, flat rates and tiered tariffs.

04 / BUDGET & SAVINGS

Small steps towards your goals

A monthly budget and a daily allowance based on what’s left. Separate savings goals with a history of deposits and withdrawals.

YOUR RECORDS STAY YOURS

No account.
No unnecessary noise.

Your records are stored on your device. The app has no advertising or analytics. You can create a backup and protect it with a password.

Exchange-rate updates use the internet. Voice entry uses your device’s speech recognition service; see the privacy policy for details.
