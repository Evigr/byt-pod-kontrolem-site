2. What the app is

“Home Under Control” is a personal record-keeping tool: household deadlines, recurring payments, expenses and meter readings. The app stores information you entered yourself, counts days and amounts and creates reminders on your device.

The app is not connected to banks, payment systems, service providers, insurance companies or government bodies, receives no information from them and makes no payments. The only thing the app receives from the internet is reference exchange rates (section 4).

Make decisions with legal or financial consequences for you based on the original documents and, if needed, with a qualified specialist.

4. Calculations

Amounts and calculations in the app are for reference only.

• The meter cost estimate uses the tariff or tiers you entered yourself. Tiers apply to the usage between two consecutive readings; taxes, fees and recalculations are not counted separately. The provider’s bill may differ.
• Expense totals include only the records you entered.
• The combined total of expenses in different currencies is an approximate estimate. All expenses of the selected period are converted at the last saved reference rate, not at the rate on the day of each purchase and not at the rate of your bank or exchange office. The amounts of the records themselves don’t change.
• The app gets rates from third-party services (Frankfurter, or ExchangeRate-API when it’s unavailable). They don’t guarantee the accuracy or availability of rates; a rate may be out of date. The rate date and the time of the last check are shown next to the total. If there is no rate for some currency, no combined total is shown — amounts are given per currency.

Don’t rely on the app as your only reminder of a deadline or payment that matters to you.
